# Keep awake, updates and other tools

Small things that help: keep the computer awake for a long agent run, and stay up to date.

## Keep awake

**Settings** (the gear at the top right) → **Keep awake** keeps the screen and computer from going to sleep, like
Caffeine or Amphetamine: until you turn it off, or for 15 minutes to 8 hours.

While it's on, a coffee cup shows at the top right. **Click** it to allow sleep again; **right-click** it to change
the time.

Handy when an agent works for an hour and you want your phone to stay connected to it.

## Updates

DevCat looks for new versions on GitHub and offers them in a popup: **Update and restart**, **Later** or **Skip this
version**. Updates are signed and checked before they're installed.

Check now with *Check for updates…* in the project menu, *DevCat → Check for Updates…* on macOS, or by clicking the
version in the status bar. See [Install and update](install.md#updates).

## Open files from your system

DevCat can be the app that opens your code and text files, and `.ovpn` VPN files. See
[First steps](first-steps.md#open-files-with-devcat).
